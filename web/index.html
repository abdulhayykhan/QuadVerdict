9. Scientific Methodology & Limitations

Experimental constraints, mathematical safeguards, and honest evaluation disclosures.

Nested Cross-Validation Safeguards

Hyperparameters are tuned exclusively on inner 3-fold CV. Performance is evaluated strictly on unseen outer test folds across 5 splits with 3 repeats (15 folds total).

Nadeau-Bengio Significance Correction

Standard paired t-tests severely underestimate variance in cross-validation due to overlapping training sets. We apply the Nadeau-Bengio corrected variance term σ2 × (1/k + ntest/ntrain) followed by Holm-Bonferroni correction.

SVM Computational Subsampling Disclosure

Kernel RBF Support Vector Machines scale with O(n2) to O(n3). Per TRD §4.2, SVM is trained on a stratified subsample of at most 8,000 rows per fold inside training partitions, then calibrated with sigmoid Platt scaling.

Asymmetric Cost Optimization

Credit default evaluation with standard accuracy or 0.50 cutoff assumes equal loss for defaults and false alarms. In reality, an unrecovered charged-off loan costs substantial principal, while a rejected creditworthy applicant costs modest opportunity margin.

Single-Dataset Limitations

The benchmark is grounded in the 2005 Taiwanese credit card defaults dataset (30,000 records). While rigorous, conclusions reflect consumer credit dynamics of that regional macroeconomic environment.

Untouched 20% Hold-Out Set

A 20% stratified hold-out split (6,000 records) was sealed before any pipeline building or nested CV. It was evaluated strictly once post-hoc to verify agreement with nested CV estimations.
